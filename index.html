The Shop

A chair, not
a conveyor belt.

Sheger means the high ground. That is the idea. You come in off Edgewood, you sit down, and for forty-five minutes nobody needs anything from you except to hold still.

Six chairs. Six barbers who have been doing this longer than most of their clients have been getting haircuts. No upsell. No rotating you through whoever is free. You pick your barber, you keep your barber.
